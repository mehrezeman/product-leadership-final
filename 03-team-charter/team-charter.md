# Team Charter: Meridian Foundations

> Module 3 · Set the Habits That Make a Team Faster

## 1. What We Own

> **We own:** The Foundations field loop in the existing mobile client. That covers photo markup and one-step send, the 4-field daily log, schedule-change alerts, then offline auto-tagging, the form-factor call and the self-writing log. We also own the field win and kill signals, including the 6-week kill reviews, and the guardrail of zero required fields added to the field client.
>
> **Out of scope:** Procore sync for the 2 clients belongs to Platform engineering, with Customer success owning the client relationships. Renewal-driven enterprise features and the executive KPI dashboard belong to PM 2 and Enterprise sales. The Later bets (subcontractor portal, time tracking, web refresh, weather, AI RFI drafting, data model migration) stay unstaffed until the quarterly strategy review funds one.
>
> **Joint calls:** Adding any required field to the field client needs PM 1, UX research and me to agree before anyone builds it. Building a separate foreman app needs PM 1, the mobile engineering lead and UX research, and only after the 4-week test results are in. Taking mobile engineering capacity for anything outside the field bets needs PM 1 and PM 2 to agree and the cost to field dates stated in writing.

## 2. How We Decide

> **Who decides scope:** PM 1 decides feature and scope for the field client against the win and kill signals. PM 2 decides scope for the enterprise roadmap but cannot draw on mobile engineering without a joint call. I decide whether a field bet continues or stops at the 6-week review, and the engineering lead gives estimates but does not set priority.
>
> **How cross-team conflicts escalate:** Whoever is pushing a request (usually PM 2 or Enterprise sales) posts a one-page ask in the Foundations channel with the customer, ARR at risk, renewal date and engineer-weeks needed. PM 1 and PM 2 have 2 business days to settle it, and if they don't, I make the final call by end of day 3. Absent a decision by then, the field bet keeps its capacity, and any renewal work that wins is swapped in for field work, not added on top.
